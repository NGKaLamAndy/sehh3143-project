# sehh3143-project
SEHH3143 Group Project Statement
Online Food Ordering and Delivery Services

## The Scenario
A start-up IT company is planning to develop a software app that can provide online food ordering
and delivery services to support the operations of small and medium sized restaurants.
As an initial phase, your project team is required to choose a target restaurant (small or medium
sized) and analysis the potential problems or limitations of its current ordering and delivery
services, such as shown in the following scenario ( https://youtu.be/HMpCwDTmSAM ). Your
team should also employ the Software Engineering approach to specify the requirements, design
the software architecture, and develop a prototype of the system for the target restaurant.

## Specification of the system
This is a user interface design of the system prototype, not a full system.
